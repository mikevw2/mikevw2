## Before you sell: your 30-minute expert review

You're the credibility behind this product, so check these points first:

- [ ] **Hazard Analysis (sample rows):** the dairy examples and decision-tree answers match how you'd defend them to an auditor. The sample marks the allergen and post-pasteurization steps as CCPs through the Codex tree. Many plants handle these as allergen or sanitation preventive controls instead, so adjust if you prefer.
- [ ] **CCP-PC Plan (samples):** 161°F / 15 s HTST and the metal-detector test-piece sizes read correctly to you.
- [ ] **Setup → CIP limits:** the defaults (caustic 1.0–2.5 % at ≥ 160°F for ≥ 10 min, acid 0.5–1.5 %, sanitizer 150–200 ppm) are sensible placeholders. The kit tells buyers to replace them with their own validated values.
- [ ] **Audit Readiness:** the 57 checklist items. They're written in plain language, not copied from any scheme's code, so there are no copyright issues. Add or reword anything you'd expect an auditor to ask.
- [ ] **PRO: Allergen Matrix:** the 9 major US allergens, including sesame. The "Contains" and "Shared line" statements are planning aids; label wording still follows your regulatory review.
- [ ] **PRO: Mock Recall targets:** the defaults are 4 hours, 100 % accounted for, and every 12 months. Many customers and schemes expect a tighter time target, so pick a default you'd stand behind.
- [ ] **PRO: Pest thresholds:** the defaults per device type per month are rodent 2, bait station 1, insect light trap 20 and pheromone 5. They're placeholders for buyers to set with their pest control provider.
- [ ] **Your employer:** make sure your employment agreement allows a side business, and that nothing in the kit comes from your employer's documents. Everything I wrote is generic.

## Gumroad / website copy

**Name:** Food Safety Audit-Readiness Kit: SQF, BRCGS, FSSC 22000 & FSMA Program Workbook

**Short summary:** 12 connected food safety program tools in one Excel / Google Sheets workbook, with a live dashboard that shows how audit-ready you are.

**Description:**

Audit season shouldn't mean hunting through binders and chasing expired certificates. This kit puts your food safety program in one connected workbook. Every log feeds a dashboard that shows where you stand today.

Built by a food-manufacturing operations lead with years on regulated dairy floors, for QA managers, food safety coordinators, PCQIs and plant managers at small and mid-size facilities.

**What's inside (11 working tabs + a guide):**
- **Program dashboard:** audit readiness %, open and overdue CAPAs, overdue sanitation, 30-day CIP pass rate, supplier document issues, training compliance and EMP positives, all updating automatically
- **Hazard analysis with CCP decision tree:** likelihood × severity scoring, significance threshold and a built-in Codex decision tree that returns CCP, Not a CCP or MODIFY STEP
- **CCP & preventive control plan:** critical limits, monitoring (what, how, frequency, who), corrective action, verification and records
- **Master sanitation schedule:** next due dates and OVERDUE / DUE SOON flags calculated from frequency
- **CIP verification log:** each cycle is checked against your limits, returning PASS, FAIL (naming the failed parameter) or INCOMPLETE
- **Environmental monitoring log:** zones 1–4, with Zone 1 and open positives flagged and retest tracking
- **CAPA register:** root cause, containment, corrective and preventive action, owner, due date, days open and effectiveness check
- **Approved supplier list:** GFSI certificate expiry warnings and risk-based review dates
- **Training matrix:** expired training in red, due-soon in amber, and a % current for every employee
- **Audit readiness self-assessment:** 57 requirements across 14 program areas, with readiness scored by area and overall

**Why teams like it**
- No macros, so it runs on locked-down plant PCs and in Google Sheets
- Every limit, frequency, department and course is editable on one Setup tab
- Includes a fully worked sample plant, so you see how it works in five minutes

**Please note:** this kit organizes and tracks your program. It does not replace your certification scheme's official code, FDA regulations or a qualified PCQI / practitioner. Set every limit to your own validated values. SQF, BRCGS, FSSC 22000 and IFS are trademarks of their owners; this product is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
