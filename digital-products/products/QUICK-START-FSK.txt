FOOD SAFETY AUDIT-READINESS KIT: QUICK START
============================================

IN THIS DOWNLOAD
 - Food-Safety-Audit-Readiness-Kit.xlsx: blank, ready for your facility
 - Food-Safety-Audit-Readiness-Kit-SAMPLE.xlsx: a fully worked sample plant, so you can see how everything connects

GET STARTED IN 15 MINUTES
 1. Open the blank workbook in Excel, or in Google Sheets (upload to Drive > Open with Google Sheets > File > Save as Google Sheets).
 2. Setup tab: enter your facility name and departments, your sanitation frequencies, your VALIDATED CIP limits and your training courses and refresher intervals.
 3. Load what you already have: supplier list, sanitation tasks, employees and training dates, open CAPAs.
 4. Answer the Audit Readiness self-assessment honestly. Every "No" or "Partial" is your to-do list.
 5. Check the Dashboard weekly, or before every management review.

TIPS
 - Bold columns calculate automatically. Don't type over them.
 - Filter any Status column (OVERDUE, FAIL, CERT EXPIRED) to get an instant action list.
 - Keep one workbook per facility. Save a dated copy before each audit as a snapshot.
 - No macros, so it's safe on locked-down plant computers.
