QUICK-START GUIDE
=================

Thank you for your purchase!

WHAT'S IN THIS DOWNLOAD
 - The blank spreadsheet: use this one for your own numbers
 - The SAMPLE spreadsheet: filled with example data so you can see how everything works

OPEN IN MICROSOFT EXCEL
 1. Double-click the .xlsx file.
 2. If you see "Enable Editing" at the top, click it.
 3. Start on the "Start Here" tab.

OPEN IN GOOGLE SHEETS (free)
 1. Go to drive.google.com and sign in.
 2. Click New > File upload and choose the .xlsx file.
 3. Right-click the uploaded file > Open with > Google Sheets.
 4. Click File > Save as Google Sheets. All formulas, drop-downs and charts carry over.

OPEN IN APPLE NUMBERS
 Right-click the file > Open With > Numbers.

THE GOLDEN RULE
 Only type in the YELLOW cells. Everything else calculates on its own.

STARTING A NEW YEAR
 Save a copy of the file, clear the log tabs, and change the year on the Setup tab.
