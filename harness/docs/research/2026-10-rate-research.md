## Policy patterns

- **Delivery:** baked into price (Digital Snap, Harmony Blossom, Camdas) or free within ~10 mi then quoted (Moments in Bloom). Relocation ~$90; late-night pickup surcharges.
- **Deposits:** $50 non-refundable hold; 25–50% retainer, balance 30 days out; $100 refundable damage deposit on walls (Archipelago Events); phones use a $17 waiver vs. $1,197 replacement liability.
- **Cancellation:** full refund 21+ days out less 3% fee, free date change (Audio Guestbook Co.); none inside 30 days (Flower Petal); late return 10%/day.
- **Add-ons:** custom greeting (usually included), USB/vinyl keepsake, sign, highlights reel, video messaging, remote call-in line; neon sign, draping, greenery/color upgrades; "special rate" when bundled.
- **Seasonal:** Idaho WeddingWire promos show 10% off winter/weekday and 20% weekday discounts; venues discount April/May/October. Hold list price May–Oct; 10–15% off Nov–Apr.

## Policy template

1. **Delivery radius:** free ≤25 mi; mileage beyond; city flat fees; quote venues >100 mi.
2. **Setup/teardown:** included; arrive 60 min early; teardown by 10 pm, $50/hr after; relocation fee if moved mid-event.
3. **Deposit:** 25% retainer at booking; balance 14 days before; damage hold refunded within 5 business days.
4. **Cancellation/reschedule:** 30+ days: refund less retainer (retainer transferable 12 months); 14–29 days: 50%; <14 days: none; one free date change 21+ days out.
5. **Damage/late return:** replacement schedule in contract; late returns 10%/day.
