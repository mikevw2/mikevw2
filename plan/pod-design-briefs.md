# Print-on-demand design briefs (Printful, original designs only)

Etsy requires original artwork for POD. These are briefs for designs Claude can generate concepts and copy for; final artwork files are produced with an image tool and reviewed by you for originality. No licensed characters, brand names, fonts you don't have rights to, or trend-copied layouts. Run each name through USPTO trademark search before listing.

## Line 1: Undated planners & journals (Printful spiral notebook / journal bases)
Target: January demand, Etsy + Shopify. 12–20 listings.
1. **90-Day Habit Reset** journal: left page habit grid (5 habits × 7 days), right page 3 priorities + one-line reflection. Covers: 4 colorways (sand, sage, slate, terracotta).
2. **Undated Weekly Desk Planner**: week-on-two-pages, Mon–Sun columns, bottom "meal / move / money" strip. 3 covers.
3. **Monday Reset Pad** (notepad): weekly brain-dump + top 3 + "waiting on" box. 2 colorways.
4. **Morning Pages Lite**: 5-minute morning routine journal, 120 days. 2 covers.
5. **ADHD-friendly Daily Sheet**: time-blocked day with "only 3 things" and a dopamine checklist. 2 covers.

## Line 2: Desk mats (Printful desk mat)
Target: evergreen + WFH gifting. 8–12 listings.
6. **Quiet Grid**: faint 2cm dot grid, corner weekly mini-calendar area, neutral tones (oat, charcoal, moss, clay).
7. **Dual-Monitor Wide** version of Quiet Grid.
8. **Topographic Lines**: subtle contour pattern, 3 colorways.
9. **Daily Three**: printed "Today / Later / Done" zones on the mat.

13. **Custom monogram variant** of 10–12 (personalization lifts Etsy conversion).

## Line 4: Small gift add-ons (optional, cheap to list)
14. **Sticky note pads** matching the planner line.

## Listing copy rules
- Honest production times (Printful 2–5 business days + shipping) in every listing. Disclose Printful as production partner on Etsy.
- Real mockups from Printful plus at least one in-use photo of your sample per product.
- Price for ~40% contribution after Etsy fees (plan for 26% all-in on Offsite-Ads orders).
