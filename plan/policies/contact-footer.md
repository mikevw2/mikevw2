# Footer contact block (required for Merchant Center)

Google's misrepresentation reviewers look for a real business identity. Put this in the theme footer and on a `/pages/contact` page:

```
[BRAND] is a [LLC NAME] company
[STREET ADDRESS], Twin Falls, ID [ZIP]
Email: [EMAIL]   Phone: [PHONE]   Hours: Mon–Fri 9am–5pm MT
Shipping Policy · Returns & Refunds · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service · Cookie settings
```

Also: a Contact page with a working form, the same address and phone, and a one-paragraph About page that names you as the owner. Use the LLC's street address (a registered-agent address is acceptable if it's a real place that receives mail).
