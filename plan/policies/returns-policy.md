# Returns & Refunds

We want you to be happy with what you bought.

**30-day returns on stocked items.** Organizers and home goods can be returned within **30 days of delivery** if unused and in original packaging. Email **[EMAIL]** with your order number; we'll send a prepaid return label. Refunds go to your original payment method within **7 business days** of the return arriving at our warehouse. A **$[X.XX]** return shipping fee is deducted unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong thing.

**Made-to-order items.** Planners, desk mats and throws are printed for you, so we can't accept returns for change of mind. If one arrives damaged, misprinted or defective, send photos within **14 days of delivery** and we'll replace it free or refund in full.

**Wrong or missing items.** We'll fix it at our cost. Email us within 14 days of delivery.

**Cancellations.** Stocked items can be cancelled any time before they ship. Made-to-order items can be cancelled within **12 hours** of ordering, before production starts.

**Refund timing.** Once approved, refunds are issued within 7 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

**Exchanges.** We don't do direct exchanges; return the item and place a new order.
