# Shipping Policy

**[BRAND]** ships to addresses in the United States. We are a small, US-based shop in Twin Falls, Idaho. Items are made or fulfilled by two partners so we can keep prices fair and delivery honest:

- **Organizers and home goods** ship from a warehouse in the United States. Orders are processed within **1–2 business days** and typically arrive **3–7 business days** after they ship.
- **Planners, desk mats and throws** are made to order by our print partner in the United States. Production takes **2–5 business days**, and delivery typically takes **3–7 business days** after that.

Every product page shows the estimated delivery window for that item. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

**If something is delayed.** If we can't ship within the time we promised, we will email you with a new date and you can cancel for a full refund at no cost. If we don't hear back from you, we'll keep your order open and ship it as soon as it's ready. We follow the FTC's Mail Order Rule: we never charge for an order we can't ship within the stated window, or 30 days if no window is stated, without giving you the option to cancel.

**Shipping cost.** Flat **$[X.XX]** per order, free over **$[XX]**. The exact amount is shown at checkout before you pay.

**Address problems.** Please double-check your address. If a package is returned to us because of an incorrect address, we'll contact you to reship (reshipping fee may apply) or refund the item cost.

**Lost or damaged in transit.** If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days or the package arrives damaged, email us within 14 days of the expected delivery date with your order number and photos, and we'll replace or refund.

**We do not ship** outside the United States, to freight forwarders, or to PO boxes for oversized items (noted on product pages).
