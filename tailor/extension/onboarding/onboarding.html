    You're all set

    Every website you open will now follow your profile.

    • Click the Tailor button in your toolbar to switch it off for a site, or to open a simplified version of a page.
    • Come back here at any time to change your choices. Open this page from the toolbar button or from your browser's extension settings.
    • Pages that are already open need a refresh the first time.
    Extra protection (optional)

    Lock your profile with a passkey: your fingerprint, face, PIN or a security key. The secret that unlocks it stays inside your device's security chip, so even someone who copies your computer's files can't read your profile.

    • You'll unlock once each time you open your browser. Until then, websites look the way they normally do.
    • If you lose the passkey, nobody can recover the profile, not even us. You would set it up again.

    Your data

    Your profile is encrypted with a key created on this device. The key can't be copied out of the browser by any script, including Tailor's own.

    A downloaded copy isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.
